Shopfront is deploying to your cluster.

Check rollout status:
  kubectl get pods -w

Get the frontend's public address (may take a minute or two to provision):
  kubectl get svc frontend -w

Once EXTERNAL-IP shows a hostname (not <pending>), open it in your browser.

To peek at MailHog (order confirmation emails) without exposing it publicly:
  kubectl port-forward svc/mailhog 8025:8025
  then open http://localhost:8025
